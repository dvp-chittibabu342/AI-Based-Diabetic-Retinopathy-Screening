About RetinaGuard Workstation

RetinaGuard 2.0 is an edge-deployable, offline-first diabetic retinopathy screening workstation developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026, Problem Statement: SIH26038, Team VyuhaX).

Key Architectural Pillars:

  • Single Source of Truth: Unified AnalysisResult contract guarantees strict parity across UI, ExplainAI, and clinical reports.
  • Real Attribution Explainability: Primary Grad-CAM++ with higher-order gradients and real Grad-CAM fallback (zero fabricated heatmaps).
  • Candidate Evidence Framing: Detections framed honestly as candidate findings with localized $96 \times 96$ px visual crops.
  • On-Demand Region Sensitivity: Evaluates model sensitivity to candidate region masking without altering weights.
  • Grounded ExplainAI: Fully offline, deterministic reasoning answering 8 core screening questions strictly from verified facts.
  • Quality Gate Safety: Halts downstream grading on ungradable fundus scans to protect patient safety.
